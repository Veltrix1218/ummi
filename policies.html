Privacy policy

At Ummi Qur'an Shoes and Bags Store, we respect the privacy of our customers and visitors. This policy explains how personal information is handled when you contact us or enquire about products. We handle personal information in line with applicable Nigerian data protection law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Information you may provide

Depending on how you interact with us, you may provide information such as:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number or WhatsApp number
  • Your email address, if requested
  • Product preferences and quote requests
  • Delivery address and other delivery details, when needed
  • Messages or additional information you choose to share

How information is used

Information you provide is used to respond to enquiries, prepare quote requests, confirm product availability, discuss orders, arrange delivery or pickup, and provide customer support.

Your shopping bag

Items you add to your shopping bag are stored in your own browser so they are still there when you return. This list is not sent to us until you choose to send your order on WhatsApp.

WhatsApp communication

If you choose to contact us through WhatsApp, your communication is also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy practices and terms. Please avoid sharing sensitive information that is not necessary for your enquiry.

Information sharing

We only share personal information when necessary to handle your request, arrange a service you have asked for (such as a delivery), comply with legal obligations, or where another lawful basis applies.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect personal information and do not keep it longer than necessary for its stated purpose or applicable legal requirements.

Your privacy choices

You may contact us to ask questions about the personal information you have provided or to request a correction or deletion, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Terms of service

By using the Ummi Qur'an Shoes and Bags Store website, you agree to use it lawfully and to provide accurate information when making enquiries or placing orders.

Product prices, availability, delivery fees, and order details must be confirmed with the store before an order is finalised. We may update these terms when necessary, subject to applicable Nigerian law.

Ordering policy

Adding items to your shopping bag or sending a quote request does not confirm an order. It prepares your request so we can work through the details with you.

  • We confirm product availability, size, colour and final price with you on WhatsApp or by phone.
  • An order is only confirmed once you and the store have agreed on the items, price, delivery or pickup arrangement, and payment.
  • If an item is no longer available, we will tell you and suggest alternatives where we can.
  • Please give accurate contact and delivery details so we can reach you.

Payment policy

This website does not take online payments. Payment is arranged directly with the store after you have confirmed your order with us.

  • We will tell you clearly what is due, what it covers, and when it is due before you pay.
  • Delivery fees, where they apply, are confirmed before payment.
  • Keep your proof of payment until you have received your order.

Only pay to account details given to you by Ummi Qur'an through our official WhatsApp or phone number, 0703 008 2498. Do not pay to any other number or account claiming to be us.

Delivery policy

Ummi Qur'an Shoes and Bags Store offers delivery nationwide in Nigeria. Delivery fees and estimated arrival times depend on the customer's location and the delivery arrangement. Please contact us to confirm delivery details before finalising your order.

Pickup policy

Customers may arrange local pickup in Okinni, Agunbelewo Area, Osogbo, Osun State, Nigeria. Please contact us in advance to confirm product availability, pickup arrangements, and the appropriate collection time before visiting.

Returns & exchanges

Please check your item when you receive or collect it. If something is wrong, such as a damaged item or an item that does not match what we confirmed with you, contact us as soon as possible on WhatsApp with clear photos and your order details.

  • We review each case individually and will tell you the options available, which may include an exchange.
  • Items must be unused and in their original condition, and exchanges depend on availability.
  • Please do not send anything back until we have agreed the next step with you.

Product information & pricing

We aim to display accurate product descriptions, photographs, and prices. However, colours may appear differently on different screens, and product availability and prices may change. Please confirm the product details, size, colour, availability, and final price with us before placing your order.

Contact us

For questions about our products, orders, delivery, pickup, or these policies, contact Ummi Qur'an Shoes and Bags Store by phone or WhatsApp.
